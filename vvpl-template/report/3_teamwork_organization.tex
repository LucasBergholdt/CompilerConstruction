\section{Teamwork Organization}

% Describe how you organized the work together in the team? How did you communicate, implement and distribute tasks? \\
% Additionally, reflect on your individual learning outcomes (particularly if you worked alone).
% %If you worked alone, add the reflection of the individual report here.

Our teamwork was characterized by a distribution of tasks followed by a common briefing. The project had many separate parts, so we always had something to do. 
For instance, the visitor pattern allowed us to distribute one visitor type to each group member.
We made sure to give each other feedback, help debug and understand each other's solutions during development.
