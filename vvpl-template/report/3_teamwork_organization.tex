Our teamwork was characterized by a distribution of primary focus areas.
We made sure to regularly brief each other and discussed major design decisions as a group.
When challenges arose we helped each other and made sure to give feedback, help debug and understand each other's
solutions during development. This ensured that all group members remained familiar with the system while maintaining a clear individual focus.
